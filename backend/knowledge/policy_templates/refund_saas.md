## 1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your {{COMPANY_NAME}} subscription at any time from your account settings or by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

- **When cancellation takes effect**: At the end of your current billing period. Your access continues until the billing period ends.
- **No mid-cycle cancellations**: We do not prorate refunds for partial months.
- **Data retention**: Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted.

## 2. Refund Policy

### Free Trial
If you cancel during your free trial period, no charges are made and no refund is necessary.

### Monthly Subscriptions
Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable. Cancellation stops future billing only.

### Annual Subscriptions
Annual subscriptions may be refunded within **{{REFUND_PERIOD}} days** of the initial purchase or annual renewal if you are not satisfied. After this period, no refunds are issued.

### Exceptions
We may issue refunds at our sole discretion in the following cases:
- Significant service downtime (more than 48 consecutive hours) caused by our infrastructure
- Duplicate charges due to a technical error

## 3. Downgrade / Plan Change

You may downgrade your plan at any time. The downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle. We do not issue credits or refunds for the difference in plan cost.

## 4. Price Changes

We reserve the right to change pricing with 30 days' notice via email. Continuing to use the service after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the new pricing.
