## 1. Overview

At {{COMPANY_NAME}}, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied for any reason, you may return eligible items within **{{REFUND_PERIOD}} days** of delivery for a full refund.

## 2. Eligibility

Items are eligible for return if:
- The item is unused and in its original condition
- The item is in its original packaging with all tags attached
- The return request is raised within {{REFUND_PERIOD}} days of delivery
- The item is not from the non-returnable categories listed below

**Non-returnable items:**
- Perishable goods (food, flowers)
- Digital products or downloadable software
- Intimate apparel or swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" at the time of purchase

## 3. How to Initiate a Return

1. Email us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your order number and reason for return
2. We will respond within 2 business days with return instructions and a return authorisation number
3. Pack the item securely and ship it to our returns address (provided in the return authorisation email)
4. Once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your refund within 5–7 business days

## 4. Refund Method

Refunds will be credited to the original payment method:
- Credit/Debit card: 5–7 business days after approval
- Net banking / UPI: 3–5 business days after approval
- Razorpay wallet: 1–2 business days after approval

We do not refund cash for online orders.

## 5. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} within **48 hours** of delivery with:
- Your order number
- Photos of the damaged/defective item and packaging

We will either replace the item at no extra cost or issue a full refund — your choice.

## 6. Shipping Costs for Returns

- If the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective product): we bear the return shipping cost
- If the return is for any other reason: the customer bears the return shipping cost

## 7. Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled **before dispatch** by contacting us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Once dispatched, cancellations are not possible — please follow the return process instead.
