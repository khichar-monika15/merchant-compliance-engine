Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: August 2024

1. Returns

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for items that are unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags attached. Handloom and handcrafted products may have natural variations — these are not defects and are not eligible for return.

2. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Email returns@artisanweaves.in with your order number and photos of the item
  2. We will review and respond within 2 business days
  3. If approved, ship the item to our warehouse (return shipping paid by customer unless defective)

3. Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom-ordered or personalised items
  • Items damaged through normal wear
  • Items without original packaging or tags
  • Sale items

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will process your refund within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

  • Credit/Debit card: 5–7 business days
  • UPI / Net banking: 3–5 business days
  • Razorpay wallet: 1–2 business days

5. Damaged or Defective Items

Contact us at hello@artisanweaves.in within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will replace or fully refund at no extra cost.

6. Order Cancellations

Cancel before dispatch by emailing us. Post-dispatch cancellations are not possible — please use the return process.
