About Artisan Weaves

Artisan Weaves Private Limited was founded in 2018 with a mission to bring authentic Indian handloom textiles to urban consumers, while ensuring fair wages for the artisan families who create them.

We work with over 500 weaver families across Varanasi, Lucknow, Jaipur, Hyderabad, and Bhubaneswar. Each piece in our collection is handcrafted using traditional techniques passed down across generations.

Our Commitment

  • Fair trade pricing for all artisans
  • Only natural dyes and sustainable materials
  • Authenticity certificates for all silk products
  • 10% of profits go to artisan welfare programs
