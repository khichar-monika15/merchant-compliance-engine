Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2023

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in plain language, what we gather when you shop with Artisan Weaves Private Limited, why we gather it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to any order placed through it.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and payment details. We also collect browsing data via cookies and analytics tools.

What We Ask For, and Why

We limit what we gather to what an order actually requires. We ask for a delivery address because a courier needs one, and for a phone number because our delivery partners call before arriving. We do not ask for date of birth, gender, or any identity document, because none of those are needed to weave and ship fabric.

Card details are never collected or stored on our servers. Payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment aggregator, and card data passes directly from your browser to Razorpay. We receive only a payment reference, the amount, and a success or failure status.

Where a field is optional we say so at the point of asking. Marketing emails require an explicit opt-in that you can withdraw at any time, and withdrawing it does not affect your ability to place orders or request refunds.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order updates and shipping notifications
  • To respond to customer support queries
  • To improve our website and product offerings
  • To send promotional emails (only with your consent)

Data Sharing

We share your data with:

  • Shipping partners (to deliver your order)
  • Payment processors (Razorpay — PCI DSS compliant)
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics — anonymised)

We do not sell your personal data.

Data Retention

We retain your order data for 7 years as required by Indian tax law. You can request deletion of non-essential data at any time.

Your Rights

Under India's DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@artisanweaves.in.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and cart functionality. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
