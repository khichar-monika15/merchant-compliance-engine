Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and payment details. We also collect browsing data via cookies and analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order updates and shipping notifications
  • To respond to customer support queries
  • To improve our website and product offerings
  • To send promotional emails (only with your consent)

Data Sharing

We share your data with:

  • Shipping partners (to deliver your order)
  • Payment processors (Razorpay — PCI DSS compliant)
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics — anonymised)

We do not sell your personal data.

Data Retention

We retain your order data for 7 years as required by Indian tax law. You can request deletion of non-essential data at any time.

Your Rights

Under India's DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@artisanweaves.in.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and cart functionality. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
