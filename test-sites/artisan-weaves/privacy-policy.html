Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2024

Artisan Weaves Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Act, 2000.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & order data: name, email, phone, shipping address, order history
  • Payment data: processed via Razorpay — we never store your card or bank details
  • Usage data: pages visited, device type, browser, IP address (via Google Analytics)
  • Cookies: session, preference, and analytics cookies

2. How We Use It

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and invoices
  • Improve our website through analytics
  • Marketing emails (opt-out anytime via unsubscribe link)

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Payment Data

All payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited (PCI DSS compliant). We do not store card or bank details.

4. Data Sharing

We share data with: Razorpay (payment), logistics partners (shipping), Google Analytics (anonymised usage). All bound by data processing agreements.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Email privacy@artisanweaves.in. We respond within 30 days.

6. Data Retention

  • Order records: 7 years (GST compliance)
  • Account data: until deletion requested
  • Analytics: 26 months
