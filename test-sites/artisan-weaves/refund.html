Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: December 2023

Returns

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for any of the following reasons:

  • Item received is damaged or defective
  • Wrong item shipped
  • Significant colour difference from product photos (handloom products may have minor natural variations)

How to Initiate a Return

Email us at returns@artisanweaves.in within 7 days of delivery. Include:

  • Your order number
  • Photos of the item (showing the issue)
  • A brief description of the problem

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will process your refund within 5-7 business days. The refund will be credited to the original payment method.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom or personalised orders
  • Sale items (marked clearly at time of purchase)

Order Cancellation

Our cancellation terms depend on when you contact us. Orders may be cancelled free of charge at any point before dispatch, which is normally within 24 hours of the order being placed. Email cancellations@artisanweaves.in with your order number and we will confirm the cancellation in writing along with the refund reference.

Once an order has been handed to our courier partner, cancellation is no longer possible and the order must instead be returned under the process described above. Made-to-order handloom pieces enter production immediately and cannot be cancelled after 24 hours, because each piece is woven individually for the customer.

If a Piece Is Not as Described

We stand behind every finished piece we sell. If the fabric you receive does not match the weave, count or colour described on the product page, we will refund the full amount you paid including the original shipping charge, and we will arrange free reverse pickup rather than asking you to post the item back at your own cost.

Refunds under this section are issued to the original payment method through Razorpay. Bank processing typically adds 3-5 working days after we release the refund, and your bank, not Artisan Weaves, controls that final leg. We will share the Razorpay refund reference so you can track it with your bank if it is delayed.
