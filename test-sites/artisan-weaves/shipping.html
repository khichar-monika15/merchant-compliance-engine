Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: December 2023

Dispatch Time

Every piece is handwoven to order. Orders are dispatched from our Lucknow workshop within 3 to 5 working days of payment confirmation. During festival periods dispatch may take up to 7 working days, and we email you the revised date if that applies to your order.

Delivery Timeline

Once dispatched, delivery takes 2 to 4 working days for metro cities and 4 to 8 working days for other serviceable areas. You receive a courier partner name and a tracking number by email and SMS as soon as the parcel is handed over.

Shipping Charges

Standard shipping is Rs. 50 per order. Express shipping is Rs. 150. Orders above Rs. 500 ship free anywhere we deliver within India.

Serviceable Areas

We deliver to all Indian PIN codes served by our courier partners, Blue Dart and India Post. If your PIN code is not serviceable, checkout tells you before payment is taken. We do not ship outside India at present.

Order Tracking

Track any order from the link in your dispatch email, or write to us with your order number and we will send the current status the same working day.

Delays and Lost Parcels

If a parcel is delayed beyond the stated window, contact us and we will raise a trace with the courier partner. If a parcel is confirmed lost in transit we reship the item at no cost, or issue a full refund under our refund policy, whichever you prefer.
